Know when something in your home is recalled

Heads Up Home collects UK recalls for food, products, medicines and pet items from official sources every hour, and tells you when one matches the allergies in your household, the things you care about, or something in your cupboard.

Why it matters

A recall only keeps you safe if you hear about it. Most of the time, people don't.

10–20%

Typically only 10–20% of recalled products are returned or fixed. The rest stay in people's homes.

Source: UK Government research (BEIS), 2020

54%

Only about half of UK adults had seen any product recall or safety warning in the past two years.

Source: Office for Product Safety and Standards survey, 2022

23%

Only 23% of adults always register new electrical appliances, which is how manufacturers find you when one is recalled.

Source: Electrical Safety First and Ipsos MORI, 2017

Where recalls come from

We check these public sources every hour and always link to the original notice, so you can see it for yourself.

  • Food Standards AgencyFood recalls and allergy alerts for England, Wales and Northern Ireland
  • Food Standards ScotlandFood recalls and allergy alerts for Scotland
  • Office for Product Safety and StandardsRecalls and safety warnings for products, from toys and baby items to electricals and tools
  • Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA)Medicine recalls and medical device safety alerts
  • Veterinary Medicines Directorate (VMD)Recalls of medicines for pets and other animals

Heads Up Home is independent and isn't connected to any of these organisations. For car, van and motorbike recalls, use the government's vehicle recall checker.
