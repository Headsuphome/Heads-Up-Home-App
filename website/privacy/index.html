Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

  • We collect as little as we can. We don't ask for your name, email or location, and there are no adverts or tracking.
  • If you turn on alerts, the app creates a private account for you with no email or password. Your choices and cupboard are copied to it, so we can tell you about recalls while the app is closed.
  • Allergies you choose are health information. We only use them to find recalls for you.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can delete everything at any time in the app: My alerts, then Delete my data.

1. Who we are

Heads Up Home is an app that tells people in the UK about recalls of food, products and medicines. It's run by Connor J Cronin ("we" or "us") in the United Kingdom.

Under UK data protection law we're the "controller" of the personal information described here, which means we decide how it's used and we're responsible for looking after it. You can contact us at support@headsuphome.co.uk.

2. What we collect and why

On your phone

Everything you choose in the app is saved on your phone: the categories you watch, the allergens in your household, the items in your cupboard, and whether you're on a free trial. If you never turn on alerts, this information stays on your phone and isn't sent to us.

If you turn on alerts

Alerts need to reach you while the app is closed, so when you turn them on the app creates a private account for you and copies the following to it:

InformationWhat it's used for
A random account numberTelling your information apart from everyone else's. It isn't linked to your name, email or phone number.
Your choices: the categories you watch, the allergens in your household, and whether you want alerts straight away or as a daily summaryDeciding which new recalls to tell you about, and when.
Your cupboard: the product names, brands and barcodes you add, and any recalls you've marked as "Not mine"Checking each new recall against the things you own, and not alerting you again about something you've ruled out.
Your phone's alert address (a code that lets notifications reach this phone) and whether it's an Android phone or an iPhoneSending alerts to your phone.
Your alert history: which recalls we've alerted you about, and whyMaking sure you never get the same alert twice, and sending daily summaries.

Changes you make in the app are copied to your account a moment later. If you remove an allergen or a cupboard item, it's removed from your account too.

Technical information

When the app or this website connects to our servers, our providers automatically record technical details such as your IP address, the type of device and the time. They keep these logs for a short time, to keep the service secure and fix problems.

When you search for a recall or scan a barcode, the words or barcode are sent to our servers so we can check every recall, including older ones. They aren't saved to your account, but they may appear in those short-lived logs.

Your camera

If you choose to scan a barcode, the app uses your camera only to read it. Nothing is recorded or saved, and the camera is never used at any other time.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your location, contacts or photos.
  • Advertising IDs or analytics. The app has no adverts and no tracking.
  • Payment details. Paid plans aren't on sale yet. When they are, payments will be handled by Google Play or the App Store, and we'll never see your card details.

3. Allergy information

The allergens you choose could say something about your health, or the health of people you live with. UK law treats this as "special category" health information, so we're especially careful with it:

  • We only use it to find recalls that matter to your household. We don't use it for anything else and never share it for marketing.
  • We only hold it if you choose allergens yourself. Choosing them is how you give us your explicit consent.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your allergens in My alerts. They're removed from your account straight away.

4. Our legal reasons

Data protection law says we need a lawful reason (a "lawful basis") for each use of your information:

  • Running the app and sending the alerts you ask for: this is necessary to provide the service you've asked for (contract).
  • Allergy information: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Notifications: your permission, given on your phone. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Technical logs and security: our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and working properly.

6. Information outside the UK

Your account and choices are stored in the UK. Some of our providers, such as Expo and Google, may process information in other countries, including the USA, when they deliver your alerts. When this happens, your information is protected by safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK-US data bridge or standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete the app or use Delete my data.
  • Your account and everything linked to it: until you delete it, in the app or by emailing us.
  • Your phone's alert address: deleted automatically if we're told it no longer works, for example after the app has been uninstalled.
  • Technical logs: kept by our providers for a short time and then deleted automatically.

Uninstalling the app doesn't delete your account by itself. To remove everything, use Delete my data before you uninstall, or email us.

8. Deleting your data

In the app: open Heads Up Home, go to My alerts, scroll down and tap Delete my data, then Delete. This immediately deletes your account and everything linked to it from our servers (your choices, allergens, cupboard, alert history and your phone's alert address) and resets the app on your phone.

By email: if you can't use the app, email support@headsuphome.co.uk with the subject "Delete my Heads Up Home data". Accounts don't have names or emails, so please include your account number, which you'll find in My alerts. We'll delete your data within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct it
  • ask us to delete it
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it
  • ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it

To use any of these rights, email support@headsuphome.co.uk with your account number from My alerts. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers. Our database is set up so that each account can only see its own information, and only the people who run Heads Up Home can manage it. We also keep what we collect to a minimum: the safest information is the information we never hold.

11. Children

Heads Up Home is designed for adults looking after a household. It isn't aimed at children.

12. This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or adverts, and it doesn't load anything from other companies. Our web host keeps the standard technical logs described above.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll also tell you in the app.

14. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email support@headsuphome.co.uk.
